Radius

From sharp to fully rounded. full applies to every interactive control — buttons, chips, badges, avatars, step indicators. Avoid none outside of edge-to-edge containers.

none — 0px
sm — 8px
md — 12px
lg — 16px
xl — 20px
full — pill

Elevation & Depth

Depth comes primarily from background and border layering, not shadow. Reserve shadow for surfaces that float above the page — modals, dropdowns, toasts.

flat + border — default cards
border-strong — hover state
shadow-float — floating surfaces

Components

Core components and their states, built directly from the tokens above.

Buttons

button-primary for the single primary action on a screen. button-secondary for everything else. Never show two primary buttons side by side.

Chips

Filters, tags, and toggleable options. A disabled chip must always be paired with a visible reason, not just a grayed-out state.

Filter label Selected option Disabled — reason shown here

Cards

Primary container for grouped content and actions — one card per item in a list or grid. Hover shifts to border-strong; avoid adding shadow on hover.

For linear, multi-step flows. Filled-accent marks the current step; muted-sunken marks future steps. Always connect steps with a line.

badge-hard for confirmed, measured values. badge-inferred for directional or estimated values. Never blend the two visually — always label which is which.
